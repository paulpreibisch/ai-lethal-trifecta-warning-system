Attention sounds
================

wait-wood-01..10.wav, wait-hollow-01..10.wav
  Author:  Paul Preibisch
  Source:  Rakazo mobile app, apps/mobile/assets/sounds/ (the waiting sound
           played while a bot is thinking in conversation mode)
  License: Apache License 2.0
  Changes: converted from AAC (.m4a) to 16-bit 22.05 kHz mono WAV.

One is picked at random and played just before a spoken prompt.
Put your own .wav files in ~/.claude-attention/sounds/ to use them instead.
